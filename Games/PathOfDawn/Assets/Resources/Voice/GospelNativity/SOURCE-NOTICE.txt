NATIVITY KEY DIALOGUE — GENERIC KOREAN AI NARRATION

Generated locally and offline with the already pinned MeloTTS Korean model,
one supported generic KR speaker. No real person's voice is copied, no speaker
is impersonated, and no paid speech API or live service is required by the game.
Character dialogue is read by the same narrator; the production does not claim
different character actors created by pitch shifting.

MeloTTS source commit: 209145371cff8fc3bd60d7be902ea69cbdb7965a
Korean model revision: 0207e5adfc90129a51b6b03d89be6d84360ed323
Existing source/model notices are retained in Tools/Voice/Licenses.

The curated dialogue-lines.json stores each exact visible line, speaker and
stable quest/beat key. The runtime accepts a clip only when key, speaker and
visible text match. Source and recording hashes, settings and sizes are kept in
production.json. Old opening voices and their original manifests remain intact.

Clips are 32 kHz mono Vorbis. Signal analysis and independent Korean ASR are
intelligibility checks, not a claim of human listening or voice-acting approval.
