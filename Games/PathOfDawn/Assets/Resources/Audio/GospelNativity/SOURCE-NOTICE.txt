NATIVITY MUSIC — THE QUIET OF BETHLEHEM

Original modern illustrative composition for FirstGame, authored in
Tools/Audio/Nativity/render-birth.py. No existing Christmas carol, commercial
recording, Ori music or Fantasy Masters recording is sampled or reproduced.
The restrained flute, strings, cello and harp arrangement is an artistic choice,
not a claim to reconstruct the music of ancient Judea. No bell, reward chime or
percussion instrument is included in this score.

Instrument performance uses the existing FluidR3 GM SoundFont under the MIT
license retained in Tools/Audio/FluidR3-LICENSE.txt. The exact source SoundFont
SHA-256 and deterministic MIDI/score/clip hashes are recorded in production.json.
The previous chapter recordings remain preserved in their existing paths.
