The original portfolio MP3 files are preserved without modifying their bytes
under Tools/Audio/FmFidelity/Originals, with SHA-256, source pages and metadata
in source-files.json. Additional SOUND-I Market, Tutorial, Game1 and Game2
recordings are preserved as research sources; they are not active runtime music.

The SOUND-I portfolio recordings contain a spoken "Welcome to Sound Interactive"
ident. Runtime login/lobby/deck music is an EDIT assembled from documented
instrumental excerpts of those recordings, with 40ms incoming pre-roll joins,
loudness normalization and Ogg/MP3 encoding. It is not an unmodified recording,
a full client-ripped soundtrack, or a restoration of missing music. No speech
separation or reconstructed/replacement melody is used. Every selected interval
is listed in loop-spec.json and runtime-manifest.json.

The complete publicly supplied S.I.D-Sound battle recording remains preserved
unchanged in Originals. The active battle loop is now an EDIT: trim the normalized
recording to 0.85–59.35 seconds, then bake a 0.35-second equal-power circular
tail/head overlap (58.15-second runtime). Login similarly trims its already
assembled instrumental excerpt to 0.35–18.52952381 seconds and uses a 0.30-second
circular overlap (17.87952381-second runtime). The original low-energy intro/outro
and earlier runtime files remain in Tools/Audio/Renewal/History/before-loop-polish.
Lobby/deck bytes are unchanged. Exact recipe, hashes and decoded PCM measurements
are in Tools/Audio/Renewal/polish-loops.py and loop-polish-manifest.json.
An exact historical game patch or a complete original soundtrack is unverified.
