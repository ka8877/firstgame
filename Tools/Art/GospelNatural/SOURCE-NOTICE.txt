Gospel natural actor revision — gospel-natural-2026-10-27

The MakeHuman hm08 anatomical base mesh, eye proxy/atlas and macro-targets
are from the official MakeHuman repository:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman

The exact downloaded files are kept in Source. Their headers explicitly state
that the assets were released as CC0 in September2020. Source/LICENSE.ASSETS.md
contains the full Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal terms. The application code
license is separate; no MakeHuman application code is bundled in the game.

The young, adult and female head adaptations use the licensed anatomical mesh
with its original UVs, age/sex target data, fitted eyelids and eye proxy. They are
artistic depictions. No historically recovered portrait or measured likeness of
Jesus or Mary is claimed.

The compatible young male/female skin atlases and long01 authored hair mesh,
three-anchor fitting proxy, diffuse/alpha atlas and materials are selected
members of the official MakeHuman system asset pack, explicitly listed CC0:
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
https://files2.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip
Exact selected members, normal HTTP byte ranges, archive CRC and SHA-256 are
recorded in Candidates/source-manifest.json. The untouched source members and
their embedded CC0 headers remain in Candidates. The full267MB archive is not
bundled. Hair is fitted using its supplied anatomical proxy and cropped at
shoulder length; it is not compressed from waist length into the cheeks.

MakeHuman head UVs and Quaternius body UVs remain separate. The new skin atlases
are used only by the new anatomical head/neck; the retained visible hands/feet
keep their own texture. Hair uses double-sided alpha-clipped strands and an
opaque anatomical scalp beneath them. The runtime material manifest records
the exact atlas paths, colors, alpha cutoff and smoothness.

Source/natural-eye-edited.png is an image-generation edit of the original
MakeHuman brown_eye.png atlas: the irises were changed from saturated red-brown
to muted chestnut while retaining the two-eye UV atlas layout. The unchanged
original and its CC0 provenance remain in Source. This is an edited asset, not
an unmodified MakeHuman original or a photograph of a real individual.

The retained body/hand/foot geometry, GospelRig65bone skeleton and authored
locomotion take data originate in the previously documented Quaternius CC0
Universal Base Characters / Universal Animation Library Standard sources.
See Tools/Art/Gospel3D/SOURCE-NOTICE.txt and its Source license files.

Original .meta GUIDs, runtime content IDs, scripture and save versions are kept.
Blender art review images are distinct from actual Unity/browser validation.
