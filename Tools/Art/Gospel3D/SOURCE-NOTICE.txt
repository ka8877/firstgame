Selected source takes: Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop,
Jump_Start, Jump_Loop, Jump_Land, Interact.
Interact is presented as a nonviolent reaching/hand movement in the optional
practice area. It is not an attack against a story character or a Bible event.

This project adapts the game-ready base to clothed youth/adult story characters:
linen tunic, woven cuffs/belt, sandals, adult mantle, youth proportions,
material tinting and compatible rig retargeting. These are artistic depictions,
not a historical reconstruction of Jesus' physical appearance. The body is a
continuous skinned mesh with modeled eyes/hands/feet, not primitive proxy parts.
Only the selected CC0 Standard files are used; no paid/source/pro editions.

Tools/Art/Gospel3D/build-actors.py reproduces the adaptation and FBX export.
Tools/Art/Gospel3D/augment-actions.py adds the four action takes to the preserved
production actors and combines matching-material skinned parts. It retains all
65 bones, modeled vertices/triangles, skinning, and the named Eyes renderer.
Tools/Art/Gospel3D/verify-actions.py reimports both exported FBXs and samples
all seven takes. Its report covers Blender assets, not Unity gameplay.
Resources/Art/Gospel3D/material-spec.json defines the URP material inputs.
Generated Blender review images are art previews, not Unity gameplay evidence.
Unity animation, contact and runtime rendering require separate validation.
