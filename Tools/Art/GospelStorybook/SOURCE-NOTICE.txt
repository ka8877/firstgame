FirstGame original storybook art — 2026-11-02

book.png and scenes.png were originally generated for this game using OpenAI
image generation. They are original illustrations, not commercial Ori/Final
Fantasy game art, recovered game client files, historic photos or excavated
reconstructions. The book contains no painted Scripture text; the Unity UI
typesets only legitimately discovered verified Korean HAN passages.

Exact original paths, SHA-256, sizes and atlas UVs: sources.json.

The original full book and corrected four-scene atlas are copied byte for byte
into Games/PathOfDawn/Assets/Resources/Art/GospelStorybook. Runtime UI selects
quadrant UVs and crops inside those regions; it does not overwrite image pixels.

An initial city skyline contained anachronistic domes. Image generation corrected
it to a first-century-inspired flat-roof limestone village. The earlier full
original is preserved unchanged under History/scenes-before-first-century-skyline.png.

The places, garments and lighting are artistic scene interpretations. Scripture
quotes, summaries and expressly dramatized speech are separately identified by
the story data. No inferred childhood incident is presented as recorded fact.

All prior art, character PNGs, measured sprite layout, source pixels, meta/GUIDs,
historic evidence and previous releases remain preserved.
