Gospel NPR presentation: sources, terms and modifications
=======================================================

This notice identifies the source material selected for the GospelNpr-2026-11-11
presentation. It is a provenance/permission record, not a statement that an
import, animation, visual review, Web build or release has passed.

The canonical shipped notice is ThirdParty/GospelNpr/SOURCE-NOTICE.txt.
The original source and adaptation records are source-manifest.json,
Extracted/C-model.json, Extracted/F-model.json and actor-manifest.json in that
GospelNpr directory. Paths recorded inside these files identify repository
assets and retained validation evidence; they are not additional public-file
links. Provenance/index.json inventories the retained research archive rather
than the contents of the public distribution. Generated/adapted output must
not be mislabeled as an unchanged upstream model or as wholly CC0. The
applicable upstream terms remain attached to the corresponding original and
derived portions.

-------------------------------
Author: Quaternius. Selected source packs: Ultimate Stylized Nature and
Ultimate Modular Ruins. Their publisher pages identify the assets as CC0 1.0:
  https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html
  https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularruins.html
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Selected spatial models:
  NormalTree_1, NormalTree_2, PalmTree_1, PalmTree_2, Bush, Grass_Small,
  Arch_Round, Column_Round, Floor_Standard.
Selected maps: NormalTree_Bark/Normal, NormalTree_Leaves,
PalmTree_Trunk/Normal, PalmTree_Leaves, Bush_Leaves and Grass.

Individual selected publisher-linked Drive URLs and exact file hashes are in
source-manifest.json. Original source files remain in Source/Environment.
Preparation remaps materials, selects sensible runtime texture resolutions,
sets appropriate alpha/normal handling and applies a coherent stone palette.
Spatial placement, sizing and static batching are game presentation changes.

Original notices/evidence:
  Provenance/nature-License.txt
  Provenance/ruins-License.txt
  Provenance/quaternius-ultimate-stylized-nature.html
The nature notice has an "Ultimate Platformer Pack" header and the ruins notice
uses a generic "LowPoly Models" header. Those preserved headers are not used
as proof of the exact pack names; the publisher pages and selected source-file
links distinguish the packs. No unselected pack character/animation is included
merely because it appears on a source webpage.
