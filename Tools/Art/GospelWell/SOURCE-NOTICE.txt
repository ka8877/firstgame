Gospel draw well and travelling oil lamp
Revision: gospel-toon-2026-10-27

The masonry, coping, shaft lining, timber winch, crank, winding rope, hollow
staved bucket and ceramic travelling lamp are original authored geometry.
build-well.py is the editable source. No commercial game model or recovered
proprietary client source is included in these new assets.

cedar-albedo.png and cedar-normal.png are original deterministic small grain
maps authored with make-cedar.py; their seed, bytes and hashes are recorded in
cedar-manifest.json. Normal convention: OpenGL +Y, no Unity green-channel flip.

Original official API published surface-preview JPEGs remain unchanged in
Games/PathOfDawn/Assets/Resources/Art/Gospel3D/TempleMaterials/limestone-albedo.jpg
and limestone-normal.jpg. Exact URLs/hashes/2048px size are recorded in
Tools/Art/GospelTemple/manifest.json. These are surface-preview images, not a
claimed recovery of the full-resolution archive. The source normal is DirectX;
the existing Unity importer flips green and remains unchanged. Only the
Blender preview material graph converts the green convention without editing
source pixels.

The former Poly Haven stone-fire-pit-derived water basin and all its sources
remain preserved in GospelAuthored/GospelAuthoredProps. It is no longer used
as the active courtyard well. Existing good authored shade trees and shrubs
are unchanged.

These are illustrative game props, not a measured archaeological restoration
of a particular historical well, Temple courtyard, or first-century winch.
Asset reimport/render checks do not establish Unity execution, physical-device
performance, user approval or full Bible campaign completion.
