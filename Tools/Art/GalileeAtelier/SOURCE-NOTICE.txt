The Way of Jesus · principal characters and the Galilean village

Five original, AI-assisted portrait paintings were made for the boy Jesus, Mary, Joseph, the adult Jesus and the teacher. Their painted features are fitted to expressive 3D faces and to the in-game dialogue illustrations. The grooms, irises, garment dyes, embroidered bands, travelling mantles, leather accessories, olive grove, village layout and distant panorama were created or adapted for the game.

Human anatomy, topology, rig weights and expression targets use MakeHuman HM08 and MPFB2 art, CC0. The boy's layered hair uses Hair 05 by culturalibre, originally by swift502. Mary's underlying skin uses Midtoned Female by CallHarvey3D. The adult Jesus skin uses Light Skin Male Bronze by Toigo. The head and exposed-limb maps contain the islands used by the game.

The Unity project contains the ready-to-render meshes, textures, portraits and materials. To rebake the fitted cast and village geometry, extract Tools/Art/GalileeAtelier/fitted-cast.zip to an external directory and set FIRSTGAME_ATELIER_INPUT to that directory. In the Unity Editor invoke GospelSculptedCastBaker.Bake, GospelPrincipalCostumes.Bake and GospelGalileeLandscapeBaker.Bake in that order. All three Editor classes are in FirstGame.Editor. source-manifest.json records SHA-256 hashes and source URLs.

For clothing pattern edits, run fetch_sources.py against the same directory and run couture.py with NumPy, SciPy and Pillow. The exporter uses the saved character bind landmarks and relaxed poses and writes editable fabric maps under working-textures. The prepared archive retains the final geometry inputs and original portrait plates. Rebaking uses the finished textures in the Unity project.
