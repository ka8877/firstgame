Fantasy Cards / Fantasy Masters client reference art
The native entry painting and unwritten window/button ornaments are sampled
from an actual Ver5.73 client capture in2011-08-03 renewal coverage:
http://imgnews.naver.net/image/5053/2011/08/03/20110803161338_3c81e997.jpg
Independent primary renewal coverage: https://www.ggemguide.com/news/143073/
The2017 client barmaid portrait and unwritten frame/button ornament strips
come from the dated live-video frame documented in:
 Docs/Research/FmChronicleSources.md
 Tools/Research/ClientMotion/selected-late-client-interface-art.json
The source PNG/JPEG bytes are unchanged; live controls are rebuilt in Unity.
Captured credentials, names, chat, room controls, rating badge, version and
cursor are not presented as live UI. Small painting omissions use runtime UV
composition; no claim is made that the resulting artwork is a clean original
painting. Identical2014–18 login art and a late native shop are not established.
Original artwork/copyright remains with its respective owners. Rights-holder
redistribution permission is not established; no ownership or license clearance
is claimed. These are reference fragments for the requested reconstruction.
Detailed source identity, exact SHA256 and era limitations are preserved in:
 Tools/Research/ClientMotion/selected-native-entry-art.json
 Tools/Research/ClientMotion/selected-late-client-interface-art.json

Galmuri11 bitmap UI font
Galmuri11 v2.40.4 is an independently licensed SIL OFL1.1 alternative, not a
claim about the original game's font. Exact source TTF remains unchanged under
Games/FantasyCards/Assets/Editor/Fonts; only the generated static bitmap atlas
is needed by the player. See the accompanying ClientMotion-FONT-NOTICE.txt for
copyright, immutable source identity and the full SIL Open Font License.

Gospel neutral characters
The selected neutral character family consists of direct Blender renders of
CC-BY3.0 HD human models by Clint Bellanger, with project-authored materials,
child proportions, facial/hair/garment details and separate idle/walk/run poses.
See the accompanying Neutral-Characters-SOURCE-NOTICE.txt and modern Flare
CREDITS/LICENSE documents for the exact model paths, attribution, source URLs
and adaptation terms. The original Flare engine/gameplay code is not included.
The earlier combat-stance PNG layers are preserved as development history;
LegacyResourceIsolation excludes those layers from the selected neutral Web
build and restores their exact bytes/GUIDs after building. Their original
notice remains in Tools/Art/ClientMotion/History/SOURCE-NOTICE-FlareLayers-v1.txt.

This notice records source/provenance, not a visual approval, full original
parity claim, Native/Web pass, or a claim that an unverified build is deployed.
