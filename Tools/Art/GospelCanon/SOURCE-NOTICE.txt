CanonArt original game illustrations

The child and adult walking atlases, four character portraits/full figures and open Bible artwork were generated for this project with OpenAI image generation in response to the user art direction. Exact generated pixels are retained; originals.json records source paths, dimensions and SHA-256. Layout JSON only describes frame bounds and feet pivots. Existing earlier artwork and GUIDs remain preserved. These are artistic reconstructions, not historical portraits.

Dawn Scripture Serif derives from Noto Serif CJK KR under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The subset was renamed; exact source, modification and notices are in font-source.json and NotoCJK-font-copyright.txt. Scripture text remains the existing approved Korean catalog, with its existing source attribution.
