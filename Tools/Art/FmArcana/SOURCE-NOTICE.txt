Fantasy Masters Arcana foreground hero illustration
Revision: fm-arcana-2026-10-27

hero-duo-original.png is a newly generated original fantasy illustration made
with the image_gen tool for this project's requested subculture title/lobby
direction. It depicts two fictional adults. It is not recovered Fantasy Masters
client art, a scan of an original card, an image of a real person, or a copied
character from another game.

The generation prompt and source/runtime hashes are preserved alongside it.
The runtime PNG is a lossless re-encoding with exactly the same RGBA pixels;
its transparent background was generated by the image tool, not removed later.

Original Fantasy Masters card illustrations remain separate source materials
under the existing ClassicCards provenance. This new foreground illustration
does not replace, relicense or claim to restore those original card artworks.

The earlier FmDetail landscape is retained as the background. The UI composes
the transparent foreground over it. No third-party game logo or character is
included in this new foreground asset.
