Nativity original development artwork

book-spread.png was generated with the image_gen tool for this project. It is original development artwork, not an Ori or Fantasy Masters asset and not a recovered historical photograph. The physical Bible spread is a modern reading-interface treatment, not a claim that such a bound book existed in the story era.

The original PNG remains under /workspace/generated_images. Tools/Art/Nativity/Sources and the runtime Resources copy preserve its exact bytes, dimensions, and color channels. No raster edits, resizing, compositing, or text painting were applied. Engine UI supplies all readable text. SHA, dimensions and stable Unity GUID are recorded in manifest.json.

opening-scenes.png is an original generated four-panel interpretation of Gospel narrative moments. The top-left and top-right scenes contain no infant, the bottom-left depicts Joseph asleep, and the bottom-right depicts visitors with Mary and a young child in a house. The artwork is not historical proof of appearances, exact architecture, number of visitors, sequence or sleeping arrangements. Runtime ArtKeys/UV quadrants are declared in manifest.json. Original pixels remain unchanged.
