# GarmentsV1 source handoff, 2026-11-18

`Firstgame.GospelNpr.Editor.GospelCharacterGarments.Apply(actor, id, necklineMaterial = null)` is an Editor-only addition after V5 tailoring and character refinement/beard, before Root material polish. It adds `<id>__HumanNecklineV1` to JesusChild, JesusAdult, Mary, Joseph and Teacher. Mary receives the plain round shoulder yoke; the four male actors also receive a narrow front placket. Child/adult proportions and the existing belt, clothing silhouettes and movement remain owned by the actor refinement pipeline.

The mesh is built from a BakeMesh sample of the actual drawn `<id>__WarmSkin` submeshes of `<id>__Body`. It measures the actual Humanoid Neck, Chest and UpperArm positions and neck/torso skin bounds, projects each yoke point onto the sampled skin and rejects a missing intersection. This avoids assumptions about original vertex order or identity bindposes, and avoids sampling unused retained Body vertices as visible geometry. The outer layer clears sampled skin by 6 mm; the closed inner layer is 2.5 mm inward. Placket is another closed thin component touching the outer yoke. Skinning uses the actual Chest/Neck/LeftUpperArm/RightUpperArm transforms, normalized full weights, and corresponding real bindposes. Closed geometry alone does not establish visual fit during motion.

New assets live under `Assets/Art/GospelCharacter20261118/GarmentsV1/<id>/`. Original and V5 mesh/material objects are never modified. A same-path existing derived mesh is reused only when its full mesh hash matches; different existing content causes an explicit failure. The material helper clones the supplied neckline material, actual fitted linen or original linen; Root subsequently applies its derived surface materials. Source Body hash and bone palette are checked after addition. New UV0 and recalculated tangents are required to be complete, finite and nonzero for Root's normal-map polish.

`Report` exposes `renderer`, mesh/source asset paths and hashes; actual `sourceSkinTriangles`, `sampledVertices`, `vertices`, `triangles`, `placketTriangles`, `indexCount`, `boundaryEdges`, `nonManifoldEdges`, `maximumInfluences`; measured `neckRadiusX`, `neckRadiusZ`, `shoulderHalfWidth`, `thickness`, `clearance`, `actorBounds` and `maximumBindRoundTripError`; and source-preservation, closed-mesh and tangent checks. `actorBounds` is the new mesh bounds in actor coordinates, not the complete actor's bounds. `sampledVertices` counts distinct drawn skin indices. `nativeMotionAndVisualReviewCompleted` intentionally remains false: successful construction and geometric checks are not a native visual approval.

Only source was authored and read. No Unity/Blender generation, test execution, asset writes, Git or network operation was performed by this agent. Root must compile and run on the actual five actors, inspect front/side/turning/walk poses and world visibility, verify no face/clothing intersection or floating collar, and preserve original-source reference/hash checks before publication. Missing projection/neck sections must remain a visible failure rather than be labelled a successful fit.
