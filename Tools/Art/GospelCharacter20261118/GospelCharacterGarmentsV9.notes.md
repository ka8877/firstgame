# Anatomical-distance cloth seam guard, source handoff 2026-11-18

V9 preserves V8 and retains class/API `GospelCharacterGarmentsV2.Apply`, the output root, mesh/renderer identities and source-protection rules. The outer seam no longer treats the UpperArm joint's half-width as the maximum position of the real cloth surface.

The actual neck-to-chest bone distance is measured in the same actor coordinates as the sampled source geometry. It defines a finite positive maximum 3D inner-to-outer seam span. The radial upper bound is `innerRadius + span`, a necessary bound derived from that distance; the existing 6 mm physical lower radial band and measured Y band remain. After finding the exact nearest point in each analytically clipped actual cloth segment/polygon, a candidate farther than `span²` is recorded and excluded while other cloth triangles continue to be considered. Since that point is already the closest point in the clipped convex region, rejecting it excludes a region with no point inside the distance sphere. Sphere boundary and derived radial boundary are inclusive consistently. The final source-normal/finite/physical gap checks remain.

The report adds actual anatomical span, per-angle outer distance/span and their maximum. Failure cut records include candidate distances/ratios and the anatomical-distance rejection reason. Inner `.65` shoulder containment, source height-plane search, weld tolerance, barycentric/source provenance, original height band, fabric loft, thickness, closed mesh, four-bone weights, bindposes, UV/tangent and all original mesh/hash checks are unchanged. No step search, arbitrary Y, skin outer fallback or enlarged numerical tolerance was introduced.

Only source was read/authored; no runtime, compiler, test or actual evidence was read or executed. Root must generate and inspect the actual complete actors and their movement before visual approval. The distance guard and nearest-source selection bound the candidate region but do not establish animated attachment or visual continuity.
