FIRSTGAME ORIGINAL SOUNDTRACKS

Starlight Overture / 별빛의 서곡
Dawn on Galilee / 갈릴리의 새벽

Original musical compositions and arrangements created for FirstGame with
AI-assisted development. Their scores are in Tools/Audio/Scores and can be
re-rendered with Tools/Audio/compose-soundtracks.py. No existing MIDI, melody,
commercial game score or recorded performance was used as a source.

The performances use the FluidR3 GM orchestral instrument samples, rendered
offline through FluidSynth. The SoundFont is a build tool and is not shipped
inside the game. Music samples are covered by the following MIT permission.
FluidSynth's software license does not change the license of its audio output.
