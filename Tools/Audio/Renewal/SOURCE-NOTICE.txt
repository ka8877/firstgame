Fantasy Masters remaster sound presentation — fm-renewal-2026-10-29

Nine new sound effects are deterministic original synthesis created for this project.
Generator: Tools/Audio/Renewal/render-sfx.py
Manifest: Tools/Audio/Renewal/sfx-manifest.json
They use filtered noise, damped oscillators, paper textures and early reflections.
They are not extracted Fantasy Masters client recordings and include no speech,
third-party samples, remote audio service or gameplay random-number use.

Original publisher source recordings remain unchanged under Tools/Audio/FmFidelity.
The active title and battle files additionally trim their low-energy edges and
bake a short equal-power circular overlap for looping. Previous runtime files
are preserved in History/before-loop-polish. See polish-loops.py and the exact
loop-polish-manifest.json for source hashes, intervals, output hashes and PCM
measurements. Lobby/deck are unchanged. This edit does not recover missing music
or establish an exact final-game-patch soundtrack.
